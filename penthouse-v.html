Private House Bratislava, Slovakia 2013 325 m²

Penthouse V

Loft "Butterfly" (Dlhé diely)

A penthouse with fluid spaces and views over the Danube meander and surrounding hills. Two sculptural cores organise the living, dining, and sleeping spaces, with a separate guest room. The terrace opens towards the floodplain forests and Austria, with a jacuzzi, sauna, and panoramic outlook.
